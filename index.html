AI Toolkit / what I actually build

Tailor-made skills, plugins and agents.

Off-the-shelf AI gives generic answers. The value is in encoding your process, your file naming, your checklists, your edge cases, so the assistant does the work your way, every time, without being re-explained.

Custom Skills

One command instead of one hour

A skill packages a real procedure: the inputs, the rules, the format of the output, so a recurring task runs identically whoever triggers it.

  • Document intake & validation
  • Report generation in house format
  • Hebrew RTL documents that don't come out scrambled
Plugins & MCP

Connected to the real systems

Skills bundled with the connectors they need, installed once and shared across a team, so the assistant reads and writes where the work already lives.

  • Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, CRM
  • Custom MCP servers over internal APIs
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1 for the glue
Agents & Automation

Work that happens without you

Scheduled runs and event-driven flows that do the routine pass on their own and only surface what needs a human.

  • Scheduled ingest and follow-up runs
  • Zapier / webhook orchestration
  • QA angle: monitoring, regression, release checks
